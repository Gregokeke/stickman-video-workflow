If you stopped eating sugar for two weeks, which may sound like torture for most sugar-addicted ghouls, but if you do, this is what would actually happen. First, you're going to lose your pathetic cravings for sugar, and you'll finally be able to go longer than two hours without food. Why? Because eating sugar spikes a hormone called insulin to clear that sugar out of your blood, triggering a violent blood sugar crash right after that tricks your brain into thinking you're starving to death when you literally just ate. Cut the sugar and your blood sugar stabilizes, while also starving out the freeloading gut bacteria in your stomach that hijack your nervous system to beg for junk food. Next, you're going to have a lot more energy. Actual energy, not a 20-minute sugar high before you feel like a sedated sloth. Because once your insulin stays low, it unlocks your fat cells, forcing your body to tap into your own fat reserves to produce steady energy. Naturally, you're going to lose more weight once your body finally starts cannibalizing the fat you've been storing since 2021. If you've been depressed or crippled with anxiety, you're going to feel noticeably more up. Because those constant blood sugar crashes flood your system with cortisol, your main stress hormone, meaning half your existential dread is really just a diet problem. Plus, sugar physically inflames your brain tissue. Cut it out and you'll start thinking better with real concentration and focus instead of staring blankly at your screen for 40 minutes. You're also going to notice your skin is a lot better and actually has a healthy glow instead of looking like an embalmed corpse. Because sugar causes glycation, a process where sugar molecules glue themselves to the collagen proteins holding your face together and literally rot them from the inside. Finally, throughout your entire body, there's going to be way less inflammation and pain. Because you stop forcing your immune system to pump out inflammatory chemicals called cytokines. So your knees might finally stop sounding like bubble wrap every time you stand up.
