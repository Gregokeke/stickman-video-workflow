# Scientific identity references
Insulin: two peptide chains, A21/B30; molecular identity reference https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4INS . Protein ribbon illustrations are schematic, not atomic-resolution structures.
Cortisol: hydrocortisone, C21H30O5, steroid four-ring framework. https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5754 .
Collagen: three intertwined polypeptide chains, not DNA. https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1CAG .
Cytokine identity: IL-6 is an example, not the sole cytokine. Four-helix bundle with connecting loops; https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1IL6 .
