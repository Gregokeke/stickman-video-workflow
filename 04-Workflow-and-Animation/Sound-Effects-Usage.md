# Sound effects — usage entry point

The complete current instructions are workflow sections 10 and 19. Extract `../11-Sound-Effects/Sound-Effects-Pack-v2.zip`, read its README, use Manifest.json/Index.csv and audition with Listen.html. Use WAV for editing and the original whoosh as one suitable variation. Match audible attacks to actions, retain tails, keep effects prominent and purposeful, preserve speech, and record cues. No music by default.

The pack contains 72 named clips (WAV and MP3 copies of the same clips), drawn from three supplied compilations plus the original whoosh. C01 is gear, as identified by the user. Descriptive names and tags are not a new license.
