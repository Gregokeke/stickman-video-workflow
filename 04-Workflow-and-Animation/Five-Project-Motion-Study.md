## Lessons promoted to production rules

- Visible, semantically correct source features govern related-object extraction; children clear their source and settle separately.
- Independently addressable parts/masks, correct pivots and shared coordinate registration enable explanatory motion.
- Readable holds and clear gaps matter as much as smooth easing. Check every transition frame in future production, not only held frames.
- Intentional grips, pointing, mechanisms and brief extraction overlap need explicit time-bounded exceptions; accidental contact and delayed opaque exits fail.
- Presenter retargeting begins with the new subject and follows its real transformed feature; character scale/face/grip stay readable.
- Local timing is already demonstrated by faster-whisper scripts in Brainwave, Kiwi and Leg. Reuse valid timings or transcribe/align locally; ElevenLabs is TTS only.
- A and D alone are approved ordinary-character references. Anatomy bodies stay hairless/unclothed stylized stickmen, while explanatory props look 3D. Ordinary figures can also be hairless or normal stickmen; retain the same underlying body shape and proportions.
- Count newly created independent assets separately from appearances, reuse, presenters and clipped parts. Archive prose and storyboard counts can use different definitions; do not inflate original-art counts with duplicates or layer splits.

Existing global narration, sound, illustration originality, provenance, scientific accuracy and full-study requirements remain applicable. No new instructor poses were generated in this documentation update.
