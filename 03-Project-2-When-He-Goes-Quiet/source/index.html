When a man feels unappreciated,
he doesn't always
pack a bag and leave.
Instead,
he just puts his soul
on mute.
He stops repeating himself,
not because he magically
ran out of things to say,
but because he realized
talking to a brick wall
is a terrible use of oxygen.
He stops asking for effort,
not because he doesn't want it,
but because begging someone
for the bare minimum
is a humiliation ritual
he's finally too exhausted
to perform.
And here's the darkest part.
When he goes quiet,
you'll probably think
everything is fine.
You'll mistake his silence for peace
and think, nice,
we haven't argued in weeks,
spoiler, he isn't at peace.
He's just quietly hosting
a funeral for his feelings
in his head.
You don't lose him
all at once
in some big dramatic breakup.
You drain him,
moment by moment.
Until one day,
you look over and realize
the man who once
gave you his all
is now only giving you
what's left of
his emotional scraps.
He stops showing up
the way he used to,
the little favors vanish,
the check-ins dry up,
and he withdraws quietly
while the emotional space
between you
turns into a canyon.
His body might still
be sitting on your couch,
but make no mistake,
the version of him
that actually loved you
is already dead.
