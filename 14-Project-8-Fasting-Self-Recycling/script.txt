When you fast, what actually happens? Well, you stop giving your body an energy source, and even though you stopped eating, your body didn't. It's just eating you instead. Yep, you heard that right. Once your blood sugar and insulin drop, your body flips on a survival switch called autophagy, which literally means self -eating, and starts hunting down senescent cells. These are broken, retired cells just squatting in your body rent free that can no longer do their job. They're technically alive, completely useless, and just sit there rotting and leaking inflammation. Basically, that one unemployed roommate who refuses to move out, and the exact reason you wake up feeling puffy, stiff, and 80 years old in your 20s. To get rid of that inflammation, your body literally cannibalizes them, stripping their junk parts down into amino acids, raw protein building blocks, and burning them for fuel, which finally clears out your brain fog, depuffs your face, and stops your joints from aching for no reason. Once your body finishes eating its own garbage, it flips into healing and repair mode, firing up your stem cells to replace those deadbeat cells with fresh, brand new, healthy ones, meaning tighter skin, faster recovery, and a gut that isn't constantly at war with itself. It's wild that your body does all that just because you locked your jaw for a few hours. Honestly, it's divine.
