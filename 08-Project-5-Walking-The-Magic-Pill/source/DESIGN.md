# Walking — The Magic Pill

## Style Prompt
Expressive rounded red stickman illustrations with sparse black contours on a bright white vertical field. Dry, sarcastic acting and coherent interactions carry the humour. Large readable characters lead the narrative; precise editable demonstrations take prominence only during numerical explanations. Maintain the proportions, hand language and line weight in the three attached style references.

## Colors
- #ED0B10: principal artwork and active comparison units.
- #101010: expressions, captions, axes and contours.
- #FFFFFF: canvas and negative space.
- #DE0B13: caption emphasis.
- #D9D9D9: inactive reference scaffolding, never an additional data series.

## Typography
Use the approved local Caption Bold font. Fixed upper phrase captions, at most two lines. Diagram labels remain separate editable elements with tabular numerals. Keep qualifiers beside their numbers.

## Layout and hierarchy
1080 × 1920 at 30fps. Use the actual supplied red-zone guide for review. Illustrations occupy the broad central field below upper captions; deliberate edge-cropped large avatars are permitted, while faces, chart data and cane tips stay protected. Measure actual alpha bounds. Build dominant hero and supporting prop arrangements with generous gaps. Diagrams keep a stable baseline and quiet supporting character. Avoid equal tiny subjects.

## Motion
Smooth move → firm hold → move. Large lateral entrances, diagonal transfers into a supporting position, center → side → center returns, one motivated push-in and occasional depth emergence. No bounce or idle float. Use nested wrappers so travel, scale and local action do not compete. Ordinary outgoing art starts fading about 40ms after travel begins and becomes transparent in about 300ms; review each handoff. Intentional coexistence is explicitly recorded. User's early-fade direction overrides generic HyperFrames transition defaults.

## Micro explainers
Retain axes, labels and neutral scaffolding while the relevant fill, selected category or comparison units change. Draw actual paths progressively; keep text unscaled when bars grow. Reveal ratio labels after the units demonstrate them. A held cane must point at the actual selected detail. Show 12% as a relative comparison only, not a guaranteed individual outcome or invented survival probability. Do not invent intermediate dataset values or a numeric threshold for the over-50% illustration.

## Sound
No music. Clean selected whooshes on major spatial handoffs, solid restrained impacts for punchlines and a few deliberate interaction ticks. No glitch textures, no sound on every bar/object. Preserve narration clarity.

## What NOT to Do
- No code-drawn main characters, contact-sheet substitutes, or arbitrary icons for narration.
- No continuous wobble, repeated identical entrances, opaque double silhouettes or clutter.
- No tiny avatars beside tiny charts, or stretched artwork.
- No invented numbers, labels, causal claims or off-target pointing.
- No new voice generation, time stretching, clipped words or unverified final-delivery claims.

## Checkpoints
Save the initial audio/planning package before artwork, then save each small artwork batch immediately. Save composition source before rendering and verify final MP4 and ZIP before delivery.

## Latest hierarchy correction
A complete action illustration owns its whole narrated thought. Do not add detached legs to a walking character, food beside a delivery handoff, or a flash prop that repeats a visible action. Start with one large dominant illustration; earn a second element through a statistic, comparison or new explanatory detail. A fleeting insignificant prop should be omitted. Preserve useful changing statistic supports. Select illustration counts by meaning and readability.

Use explicit center-to-side-to-center returns, retained comparison pairs and changes of prominence. Never shrink the main illustration merely because a supporting asset exists.
