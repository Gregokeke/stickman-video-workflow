Science shows that your brain and your best friend's brain literally sync up together like Bluetooth, which explains how the two of you managed to share a single functioning brain cell. In neuroscience, it's called neurosynchrony, and it's the biological reason you can finish each other's sentences, communicate with a single unhinged glance across a crowded room, and laugh at the exact same dark, twisted stuff that would get you both cancelled. See, when you spend enough time around someone, your brains actually physically align their electrical rhythms, called brain waves, using mirror neurons, which are specialized brain cells that copy and mimic the exact behavior and feelings of the person sitting next to you. When neuroscientists threw best friends into brain scanners, they found identical, synchronized electrical spikes lighting up the exact same regions at the exact same millisecond. The amygdala, which processes raw emotion, the prefrontal cortex, which handles decision making, or in your case, terrible life choices, and the neural circuits for empathy. It explains why you and your best friend are such insufferable weirdos together, because your nervous systems are literally trapped in an echo chamber. But here is the terrifying part. It also proves you need to choose your circle wisely. If your best friend is a broke, toxic disaster with zero ambition, your brain isn't just hanging out with them. It is actively downloading their broken neural wiring and rewriting who you are going to become.
