## This project's delivered files
The used cues are supplied as short WAV derivatives in assets/sfx. They were extracted from the approved project's separate effects.wav track at isolated cue timestamps, without narration, and remixed against this video's supplied voiceover. This project includes whoosh-short,whoosh,click,impact-bass-1,impact-bass-2,glitch-1 and chime. The general upstream MP3 listing above is provenance, not a claim that every listed MP3 is bundled here.


## Glow Trinity sound design
