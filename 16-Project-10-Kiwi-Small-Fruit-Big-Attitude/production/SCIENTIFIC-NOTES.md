Scope and limits: The stated approximately4g fiber and95% use the supplied script, not independently collected data. Below recommended fiber intake is not equivalent to a diagnosed clinical deficiency, and the95% figure should not be generalized as a measured global population. Visual below-goal units are schematic. Trial bars show direction only, have no numerical axis and are labeled SCHEMATIC; they do not encode invented outcome values. The original narration overstates how established the sleep evidence is; the video preserves that recording, adds no efficacy percentages and uses a schematic sleep-quality bar. Peripheral/dietary serotonin does not simply cross into the brain; the endocrine diagram depicts endogenous pineal chemistry only. The serotonin-to-melatonin route is a simplified multistep synthesis, not direct one-step conversion or proof of kiwi mechanism. Digestion scissors are an explicit script metaphor; actinidin itself is a globular protein, and the stomach/protein cleavage representation is schematic. Food vitamin content varies by size/cultivar; no exact invented vitamin-C comparison bars are added.
