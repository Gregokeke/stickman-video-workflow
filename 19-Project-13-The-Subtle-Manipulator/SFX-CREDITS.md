# Sound effects

Used original WAV effects from the user-supplied Sound-Effects-Pack-v2, drawn from the three supplied compilations plus original project whoosh. Descriptive names do not imply new licensing. Full provenance for used files is in assets/sfx/Manifest.json. No generated or substituted effects; no music. Cue attacks, peak gains and uses are recorded in sound-cues.json. Source attacks/decay tails are retained.
